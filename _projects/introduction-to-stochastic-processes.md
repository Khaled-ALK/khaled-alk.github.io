## Overview
### Introduction to Stochastic Processes (MIT 18.445, Spring 2015)

Graduate-level course at MIT taught by **Dr. Hao Wu**.

- Covered fundamental results on **Markov chains** and **martingales**  
- Skipped most technical proofs (e.g., **\(L^1\)** and **\(L^p\)** convergence, **almost sure convergence**), but focused on intuition and applications  
- Explored the power of martingale methods in **branching processes**  
- Concluded with an introduction to **Poisson processes**

[Course syllabus (MIT OCW)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-445-introduction-to-stochastic-processes-spring-2015/pages/syllabus/)


## Reading List
1. [Markov Chains and Mixing Times](https://pages.uoregon.edu/dlevin/MARKOV/markovmixing.pdf) – Levin, Peres et al.  
   Using mainly the first four chapters as a reference for **discrete-time, finite-state Markov chains**.

2. [Probability: Theory and Examples – Martingale Notes](https://sites.math.duke.edu/~rtd/PTE/PTE5_011119.pdf) – Rick Durrett  
   A good reference for results on **discrete-time martingales**.
