Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus

Notes, readings, and references for the BIMSA course Probability 3: Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus.

Overview

This is a graduate-level course on Brownian motion and stochastic calculus, following the BIMSA course Probability 3: A course on Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus taught by Yuval Peres in Autumn 2024. The aim is to develop the theory of Brownian motion, martingales and stochastic integration, and to explore applications such as conformal invariance, the law of the iterated logarithm, and geometric properties of Brownian paths. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The official BIMSA page (with registration details, videos and notes) is:

  • Probability 3: A course on Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus (BIMSA)

Prerequisites

The course assumes solid measure-theoretic probability, in particular:

  • Conditional expectation
  • Discrete-time martingales and stopping times :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Students should be comfortable with σ-algebras, modes of convergence, and basic results such as the dominated convergence theorem.

Topics / Syllabus (following BIMSA)

The topics follow closely the BIMSA syllabus: :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

  • Gaussian vectors and basic multivariate normal theory
  • Brownian motion: construction, continuity of paths, and almost-sure nondifferentiability
  • Strong Markov property and stopping times for Brownian motion
  • Multidimensional Brownian motion: harmonic functions, recurrence versus transience
  • Skorokhod embedding and the law of the iterated logarithm
  • Stochastic integration with respect to Brownian motion and Itô’s formula
  • Conformal invariance of planar Brownian motion
  • Continuous-time martingales and local martingales
  • Stochastic integration with respect to local martingales and semimartingales; general Itô formula
  • Girsanov’s theorem and change of measure
  • Basic notions of Hausdorff dimension
  • Dimension of the zero set, path and graph of Brownian motion
  • Local time of Brownian motion
  • Intersections of Brownian paths and exceptional subsets of the path

References

The main references listed for the BIMSA course are: :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

  1. Jean-François Le Gall, Brownian Motion, Martingales, and Stochastic Calculus, Springer.
    A modern, rigorous introduction to Brownian motion, continuous-time martingales, and stochastic integration, with many detailed proofs.

  2. Peter Mörters & Yuval Peres, Brownian Motion, Cambridge University Press.
    Focuses on sample-path properties, potential theory, and geometric aspects of Brownian motion, including Hausdorff dimension, local time, intersections, and exceptional sets.
